      Features

      Enable or configure key modules to tailor CLM365 functionality according to organizational requirements.


      Business Unit: Business Units categorize different segments or divisions within an organization. They are used to organize contracts and manage permissions or access levels for users based on their roles within specific business units.



      Request, Contract Settings: Configure request types, draft types, and contract types for structured contract initiation.



      Link Contracts: The Link Contracts feature allows users to associate a newly created request with an existing active contract...

      Only active contracts can be linked. Draft, inactive, or completed contracts will not be shown in the dropdown.

      Step 1: Enable the Linked Contracts Feature

      1. Navigate to Administration Settings.

      2. Scroll to the Features section and locate the Linked Contracts toggle.

      3. Switch the toggle to On and click Save.


      Step 2: Create a Request with Linked Contract

      1. Go to the Home screen and click New Request.


      2. Fill in the required fields. When you select the Counterparty, the system automatically populates the Linked Contracts dropdown with all active contracts associated with that counterparty.

      3. Select the relevant contract to link, then click Submit to complete the request.


      Step 3: View Linked Contracts After Publishing

      1. After publishing, open the parent contract used during the linking process.

      2. Navigate to the Linked Contracts tab.

      3. The child contract (newly created request) will appear here as a linked item.


      4. Click the linked contract to view its full details.


      Step 4: Use Linked Contracts in Reports

      1. Go to the Reports section and open Contract Reports.

      2. Apply the Linked Contracts filter to view only those contracts that are associated with others.

      This helps in quickly identifying related agreements and managing interdependent contracts effectively.



    Deviation:

    A deviation refers to any change or exception to the standard terms, clauses, or policies defined in a contract template. Such deviations may arise during drafting, negotiation, or approval and require careful review to minimize legal, financial, or compliance risks. For example, modifying payment terms—such as changing the schedule or amount—would be considered a deviation.

    In our application, the Deviation Task feature helps manage these exceptions effectively. It can be created from the timeline during the legal review stage or while approving the legal review. Once created, the task enables structured communication between the creator and the assigned approver, ensuring proper clarification, documentation, and approval of contract changes.

    Steps to Enable Deviation Feature

    1. Navigate to Settings in the application.
    2. Locate the Deviation toggle option.
    3. Enable the toggle to activate the Deviation Task feature.




    Steps to Create a Deviation from Legal Review Stage

    1. Navigate to the Legal Review stage of the contract.
    2. Before approving the legal review, click on the Timeline section.
    3. From the Timeline, select the option to Create Deviation.
    4. Enter the required details and submit the Deviation Task for review and approval.




    1. In the Create Deviation window, enter the required details:

    1. Title
    2. Assign To
    3. Due Date
    4. Description

    After filling in all mandatory fields, click Submit.



    Once submitted, the Deviation Task will be automatically assigned to the selected user for review and further action.





    Once the Deviation Task is submitted, it will appear under My Tasks for the assigned user. When the user clicks on the task, the Deviation panel will open, allowing them to review the details, provide comments, and take the necessary action.





    If multiple deviations are created for a contract, they will be listed under the Recent Deviations section in the Deviation panel.



    When you click on Reply, a text box will open, allowing you to enter your message. You can use this section to communicate directly with the user who created the Deviation Task.

    This feature enables clear discussion, clarification, and updates before approving or rejecting the deviation.





    After entering your message in the text box, click Save. The message will be sent to the task creator and recorded within the Deviation panel, ensuring proper communication and documentation of the discussion.





    The task creator can navigate to the section and click on the respective Deviation Task to view the message. All responses and updates will be displayed within the task, ensuring complete visibility of the communication history.





    The task creator can respond to the message by clicking on Reply within the Deviation Task. This will open the text box, allowing them to enter their response and continue the conversation within the same task.



    The assigned user can view the reply directly within the same Deviation Task panel. All responses are displayed in the task conversation, ensuring both parties can track and continue the discussion in one place.

    Once the discussion is completed and all clarifications are addressed, the assigned user can proceed to either Approve or Reject the Deviation Task directly from the same panel.



    After the Deviation Task is approved or rejected, the updated status will be displayed under the Deviation section. This allows all stakeholders to easily track the final decision and view the current state of the deviation within the contract workflow.



    Risk:

    The Risk section displays key risk details within the request panel. This information helps users assess and manage potential contract-related risks effectively.

    Risk with AI Integration When AI integration is enabled, the system automatically generates risk insights for the contract within the request panel.





    Updating an AI-Generated Risk

    From the Risk tab:

    1. Locate the AI-generated risk in the list.
    2. Click the three-dot (⋮) menu next to the selected risk.
    3. Select Edit.

      4. The Update Risk panel will open, allowing you to modify the necessary fields (such as severity, priority, assignee, or status).





    Viewing and Replacing a Clause

    Within the Update Risk panel:

      1. Click the Eye icon next to the Clause field.
    • The associated contract document will open.


      2. In the document view, click Search Alternative Clause.
    • The AI will generate suggested alternative clauses based on the selected content.
    • You can also browse and select clauses from the Clause Library .


      3. Review the suggested clauses and choose the most appropriate option:
    • Select an AI-generated alternative, or
    • Select a clause from the Clause Library.

    4. Click Update in Contract to apply the selected clause to the document.

    The contract will be updated with the chosen clause accordingly.



    5. After selecting the appropriate clause, click Update.

    • The contract document will be updated with the selected clause.
    • The associated risk status will automatically move to Mitigated.


    You can also reassign the task to another user if required. To do this, click on the three dots next to the task and select Edit. From there, choose the user to whom you want to assign the task and save the changes.

    Once reassigned, the task will appear under the My Tasks section for the selected user, and they can proceed with the task by following the same process described above.







    Risk Without AI Integration

    When AI integration is not enabled, risks must be added and managed manually.

    Adding a Risk Manually

    • Navigate to the Risk tab within the contract.
    • Click Add.
    • Enter the required details:
      1. Risk Name
      2. Clause reference
      3. Remarks (if applicable)
      4. Severity
      5. Priority
      6. Score
      7. Status
      8. Assigned To

      • 4. Click Save.









    Managing Manual Risks

    • Users can edit risks using the three-dot (⋮) menu.
    • Clause updates must be handled manually within the contract document.
    • Risk status (e.g., Not Started, In Progress, Mitigated) must be updated by the user.

    This approach provides full control over risk identification and mitigation without AI-generated suggestions.



    Viewing and Replacing a Clause

    Within the Update Risk panel:

      1. Click the Eye icon next to the Clause field.
      • The associated contract document will open.


      In the document view, click Search Alternative Clause.
      • You can browse and select clauses from the Clause Library.


      When you click Get Alternative Clause from Clause Library, a popup window appears to help you refine your search.
      Steps:
      • In the popup, select the desired Clause Type from the dropdown menu.
      • Choose relevant Tags to filter clauses by specific topics or attributes.
      • Click Submit to view the filtered list of clauses matching your criteria.
      • Browse the results and select the appropriate clause to use.






    • After selecting the desired clause from the Clause Library popup, click Update in Contract.
    • The contract document will be updated with the selected clause.
    • Click Update to save the changes to the contract document.




      Once the updated clause is saved:
    • The associated risk will automatically move to “Mitigated” status.
    • This indicates that the risk has been addressed through the clause update.
