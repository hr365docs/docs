Home Page

    The homepage of HR365 Employee Onboarding features two processes: one for onboarding employees and another for offboarding them.



    In home page menu bar:

    • My Active tasks: The tasks that need to be performed are displayed in this section.




    • My Overdue Tasks: A task that remains incomplete within a specified number of days.



      You can view the details of a task by clicking on its name.


    • Pre-Onboarding:The Pre-Onboarding Documentation delineates the process and procedures to adhere to before a new employee formally joins the organization. This phase is vital for facilitating a seamless transition for the new hire, enabling them to achieve full productivity right from their first day.



      To add a new pre-onboard, click the +New button.


      Adding Pre-Onboarding Info requires mandatory fields such as the candidate's name, email, position, and department.


      You can assign assets and licenses to the candidate. To assign assets, click the Add button to the right of the Device section. To assign licenses, click the +Add button in the Software section.



    • The system now supports automatic transition from Pre-Onboarding to Onboarding upon approval.

      • 1) Click on the employee’s name from the Pre-Onboarding list. Select the “Move to Onboarding” option.



      2)Complete the required details and confirm by clicking the “Yes” button.



      3) The candidate will then be automatically transitioned to the Onboarding stage.



    • Onboarding: From here, you can input new hire details and oversee the entire onboarding process.



      By clicking on the "+New" button, you can begin the onboarding process for a new employee. You'll be prompted to fill in basic information, and then you can proceed to the next step by clicking "Next."



      Similar to filling in basic information, you can input details step by step for job information, group membership, contact information, and current information.


    • Offboarding: You have the ability to conduct offboarding procedures for personnel directly from this section.



      By clicking on the "+New" button, you can initiate the offboarding process for an employee.




    • All Active Tasks: In this section, you'll find a list of tasks that require completion.



    • All Overdue Tasks: In this section, you'll find individuals who haven't completed their tasks within a designated timeframe.



    • Onboarded: Here is a list of employees who have been onboarded recently.



    • Offboarded: Employees who have been offboarded are listed.



    • Training This feature allows administrators to add members or employees to the system, ensuring that each added member receives an email notification containing pertinent information, including a timestamp and date stamp.

      • Position : Administrators have the ability to include positions as part of the hiring process before recruiting employees.
      1. To proceed further, click on the "Position" tab.


      2. To add a new position, click on the "+Add" button.


      3. Complete the details as per the requirements for hiring an employee.


      4. Then, Click on Save Button.


    • Approval Tracking: You can view the approval tracking for the position by clicking on the position name, as demonstrated below.



    • Probation Period Process for Onboarding Employees
      • Overview

      This knowledge base document explains the process of managing the Probation Period for employees during onboarding. The feature allows HR/Admin users to define a probation duration while onboarding an employee, automatically create a probation task for the employee’s manager, and track the probation status until completion.

      Probation Period Process

      Step 1: Add “Probationary Period” Field in Custom Form

      Navigation: Settings → Custom Forms → Edit Onboarding Form

      Steps:

      1. Open the required onboarding custom form.
      2. Search for the field “Probationary Period” from the available fields section.
      3. Drag and drop the field into the selected fields area.
      4. Save the form.

      Reference: The “Probationary Period” field should be added to the onboarding form configuration.


      Step 2: Select Probation Duration While Onboarding Employee

      During employee onboarding:

      1. Create a new onboarding request.
      2. Fill in employee details.
      3. Select the required probation duration/days in the Probationary Period field.
      4. Submit the onboarding request.

      Expected Behaviour:

      • The system calculates the probation end date based on the employee’s Date of Joining and selected probation period.
      • The onboarding request remains active until the probation process is completed.

      Step 3: Automatic Probation Task Creation

      After the onboarding request is submitted:

      • A task named “Probation Period Task” is automatically created.
      • The task is assigned to the employee’s reporting manager.
      • The task appears under: My Active Tasks and Current Tasks.

      Task Details Include:

      Detail Field Value Type / Context
      Employee Name Name of the onboarding employee
      User Email The employee's official email address
      Department / Job Title Assigned organizational department and position
      Date of Joining / Manager Official start date and assigned reporting authority
      Due Date / Process Type Calculated deadline date; Process Type is Onboarding

      Step 4: Manager Actions on Probation

      The assigned manager can review the employee’s probation and take one of the following actions:

      Available Action System Behaviour & Requirements
      1. Complete Marks the employee probation as successfully completed. The employee onboarding process is finalized and the employee moves out of the onboarding section.
      2. Reject Marks the probation as rejected. HR/Admin can take further action based on company policy.
      3. Extend Extends the employee probation period. Manager must explicitly provide an Extended probation date and a Justification/comments.

      Step 5: Employee Visibility in Onboarding

      Important Behaviour:

      • Until the probation task is marked as Completed, the employee will continue to appear in the Onboarding section, and the onboarding request status remains active/in progress.
      • Once the manager completes the probation task, the employee is moved from the onboarding stage, and the onboarding lifecycle is considered completed.
