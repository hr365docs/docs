    • Reminder Notifications — Configuration Guide

    1. Overview

    LMS 365 provides a built-in Reminder Notifications feature that allows administrators to configure automated email alerts for upcoming and overdue courses and training sessions. These notifications are triggered automatically via Microsoft Power Automate, ensuring that learners and managers are kept informed without any manual intervention.

    This document covers how to navigate to the Reminder Notifications settings, explains each available notification option, and describes how the email triggers work through Power Automate.

    2. Navigating to Reminder Notifications

    Reminder Notifications are configured from within the LMS 365 Settings panel. Follow the steps below to access this section.


    Figure 1 – LMS 365 Settings Panel with Reminder Notifications highlighted

    Steps:

    1. Click the Settings icon (⚙) from the left-side navigation bar.
    2. The Settings page opens, displaying multiple configuration sections: General Settings, App Users, General Administration, Features, AI Control Panel, and Notifications.
    3. Under the Notifications section (right column), click Reminder Notifications.
    4. The Reminder Notifications panel slides open on the right side of the screen.
    📌 Note: Only users with Admin role can access and modify Reminder Notification settings.

    3. Reminder Notifications Panel

    The Reminder Notifications panel is divided into two main categories: Course Reminder Notifications and Training Reminder Notifications. Each category has two sub-sections — Days Before (upcoming reminders) and Overdue (post-deadline reminders).


    Figure 2 – Reminder Notifications Configuration Panel

    4. Course Reminder Notifications

    This section configures automated email reminders related to courses assigned to learners.

    4.1 Days Before (Upcoming Course Reminders)

    These notifications are sent to learners a specified number of days before a course is due. Up to three separate reminder notifications can be configured.

    Notification Description How to Configure
    Course Reminder Notification 1 First reminder email sent before the course due date. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.
    Course Reminder Notification 2 Second reminder email — useful for a mid-point follow-up. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.
    Course Reminder Notification 3 Third reminder email — typically a final nudge before due date. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.
    📌 Note: In the example shown, Course Reminder Notification 1 is set to 1 day before and is toggled On. Notifications 2 and 3 are toggled Off.

    4.2 Overdue (Course Overdue Reminders)

    These notifications are sent after a course due date has passed, alerting learners (and optionally managers) that a course is overdue. Up to three overdue reminders can be configured.

    Notification Description How to Configure
    Course Overdue Reminder 1 First overdue alert sent after the course deadline passes. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.
    Course Overdue Reminder 2 Second escalation reminder for overdue courses. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.
    Course Overdue Reminder 3 Final escalation reminder for overdue courses. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.

    5. Training Reminder Notifications

    This section configures automated email reminders for scheduled training sessions. It follows the same structure as Course Reminders, with Days Before and Overdue sub-sections.

    5.1 Days Before (Upcoming Training Reminders)

    These notifications are sent before a training session's scheduled date to ensure learners are prepared and aware of the upcoming session.

    Notification Description How to Configure
    Training Reminder Notification 1 First reminder sent before the training session date. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.
    Training Reminder Notification 2 Second reminder for the upcoming training session. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.
    Training Reminder Notification 3 Third and final reminder before the training session. Enter the number of days in the input field and toggle On.

    5.2 Overdue (Training Overdue Reminders)

    These notifications are triggered after a training session date has passed if the learner has not completed or attended the session.

    Notification Description How to Configure
    Training Overdue Reminder 1 First overdue alert after the training session date passes. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.
    Training Overdue Reminder 2 Second escalation for overdue training. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.
    Training Overdue Reminder 3 Final escalation reminder for overdue training. Enter the number of days after due date and toggle On.

    6. How Notifications are Triggered — Power Automate

    All Reminder Notifications in LMS 365 are powered by Microsoft Power Automate flows running in the background. Admins do not need to manually trigger these emails — once configured and enabled, the flows handle delivery automatically.

    6.1 How It Works

    1. Admin configures the number of days and enables the toggle for the desired notification in the Reminder Notifications panel.
    2. LMS 365 stores this configuration and communicates it to the connected Power Automate flow.
    3. Power Automate runs on a scheduled basis (typically daily) and checks for courses or training sessions that match the configured day thresholds.
    4. When a match is found, Power Automate triggers an automated email to the relevant learner(s) using the configured Email Template.
    5. For overdue notifications, the same process applies — Power Automate checks how many days have passed since the due date and triggers the appropriate email.

    6.2 Notification Trigger Summary

    Notification Type Trigger Condition Recipient Triggered By
    Course Reminder (Days Before) X days before course due date Assigned Learner Power Automate (Scheduled Flow)
    Course Overdue Reminder X days after course due date (not completed) Assigned Learner Power Automate (Scheduled Flow)
    Training Reminder (Days Before) X days before training session date Assigned Learner Power Automate (Scheduled Flow)
    Training Overdue Reminder X days after training date (not attended) Assigned Learner Power Automate (Scheduled Flow)
    💡 Tip: Email content is controlled by the Email Templates configured under Settings → Notifications → Email Templates. Ensure the relevant templates are active for reminders to be delivered correctly.

    7. Configuration Steps — Quick Summary

    1. Go to Settings (⚙) from the left navigation.
    2. Under Notifications, click Reminder Notifications.
    3. For each reminder you wish to activate:
      • Enter the number of days in the input box (e.g., 1, 3, 7).
      • Toggle the switch to On (blue).
    4. Click the Save icon (💾) at the top-right of the Reminder Notifications panel to save your changes.
    5. Verify the corresponding Power Automate flow is active in your Microsoft 365 environment.
    📌 Note: If a toggle is Off or the days field is left blank, that particular reminder will not be sent, even if Power Automate is active.
